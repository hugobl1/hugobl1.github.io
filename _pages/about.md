I have completed my PhD in June 2026 and I am now a Postdoctoral Researcher at the <a href="https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-universite-cote-azur" target="_blank" rel="noopener">INRIA Centre Université Côte d'Azur</a>, in the GraphDeco team, where I work on 3D scene reconstruction and novel view synthesis.
My research focuses on Gaussian- and RBF-based volumetric representations, aiming to improve photorealistic rendering and efficiency.
I am especially interested in scalable, high-quality 3D geometry reconstruction methods and differentiable rendering pipelines.
