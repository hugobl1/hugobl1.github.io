I am a postdoctoral researcher at INRIA, within the GraphDeco Team, where I work on 3D scene reconstruction and novel view synthesis.
My research focuses on Gaussian- and RBF-based volumetric representations, aiming to improve photorealistic rendering and efficiency.
I am especially interested in scalable, high-quality 3D geometry reconstruction methods and differentiable rendering pipelines.
